Hivebreak slice 2

Make a run worth repeating: upgrades you can feel, power-ups and a Special for the big moments, a boss at the end of each sector, and a second sector that looks and plays differently.

It ships in two releases, each deployed and play-tested before the next. 2a deepens sector 1 and ends it with the Brood Mother. 2b adds sector 2 and the Hive Mind, making a run ten waves. The pictures are concept renders drawn live with Three.js at the game's 180 × 320 resolution; the shapes are stand-ins for the Blender models.

New cast

Carrier 2a

Slow and tough. Always drops a power-up.

Splitter 2b

Breaks into two mini-drones when killed.

Pickups 2a

Spread, Laser, Slow, Helper drone. 8–10 s each.

Brood Mother 2a

Wave 5. Armour plates over the core, Drone bays underneath, a faster second phase.

Hive Mind 2b

Wave 10. Rotating shield ring, beam sweeps, a desperation phase.

Upgrades, reworked

Play-testing found the upgrades weak and the run a little easy. Every pick should change how a run plays.

ChangeWhatExamples
Bigger numbersRetune the six existing upgrades so one pick is felt.Rapid fire ×0.65 interval · thrusters ×1.3 · twin shot fans out
New upgradesSix more in the pool.Homing · ricochet · combo guard · magnet · bomb on kill · overcharge
Rare tierStronger picks; one guaranteed after a boss, a small chance otherwise.Wingman · slow field · double-damage shots
SynergiesHold a pair and it upgrades into a stronger form. The pick card says when one would complete.Twin shot + piercing = Lance · rapid fire + thrusters = Afterburner · shield pulse + extra life = Bulwark
DifficultyBaseline up a step, checked with the scripted bot's win rate.Faster march · more fire · Divers from wave 2

Done means

2a

  • Five waves end in a Brood Mother fight.
  • You pick a Special at the start and can fire it from a full meter with keys, pad and touch.
  • Power-ups drop and work; the Carrier always drops one.
  • New upgrades, the rare tier and synergies are in, and the pick card shows synergy hints.

2b

  • A run is ten waves in two sectors, and sector 2 has its own palette.
  • Splitters, paired dives and split formations appear in sector 2.
  • Shields erode in both sectors.
  • The Hive Mind's three phases end the run.

Both

  • The scripted bot clears every wave and boss with spare lives; with 3 lives it wins less often than in slice 1.
  • Tests, typecheck and build pass; CI is green; it's live and you've played it.

Steps

Same way of working as slice 1: I write each release's shared types, smaller models build in parallel on separate files, a reviewer agent checks every batch and re-checks the fixes, and I play-test before each commit. Blue numbers run as parallel workers.

  1. 2a contract

    main session

    Types for power-ups and pickups, the Special choice and meter, the rare tier and synergies, the Carrier, the Brood Mother's parts and phases, the new events, and a Special-choice phase before wave 1.

  2. 2a game logic and models

    4 in parallel
    • Sonnet upgrades and tuning: retuned numbers, new upgrades, rare tier, synergies, bot win-rate report.
    • Sonnet power-ups, the three Specials, the Carrier and drop rules.
    • Sonnet the Brood Mother: movement, plates, bays, phases.
    • Sonnet Blender models: Carrier, Brood Mother, pickups, Special effects.

    Then one Sonnet worker folds them into run.ts, where they would otherwise collide.

  3. 2a renderer, UI and sound

    3 in parallel
    • Sonnet renderer: boss damage states, pickups, power-up visuals, Special effects.
    • Sonnet UI: Special choice, meter, power-up timer, boss bar, rare and synergy labels, touch Special button.
    • Haiku sound for pickups, power-ups, Specials and the boss.

  4. 2a wiring, review, ship

    Wire it into main.ts, review and fix, play-test the boss and each Special on phone and desktop, push, follow CI and Vercel, then you play it.

  5. 2b contract

    main session

    Types for sectors and palettes, the Splitter, formation behaviours, shield masks, and the Hive Mind's ring, beams and phases.

  6. 2b game logic and models

    4 in parallel
    • Sonnet waves 6–10, the Splitter, split formations and paired dives.
    • Sonnet shields: pixel masks eroded by both sides' shots.
    • Sonnet the Hive Mind's three phases.
    • Sonnet Blender models: Splitter, mini-drone, Hive Mind.

    Then the run.ts fold-in: ten waves, two sectors, a win after wave 10.

  7. 2b renderer, UI and sound

    2 in parallel
    • Sonnet renderer: sector 2 palette and red nebula, eroding shield textures, the Hive Mind with beam telegraphs.
    • Haiku sector banner, wave counter to 10, records to wave 10, new sounds.

  8. 2b wiring, review, ship

    As for 2a.
