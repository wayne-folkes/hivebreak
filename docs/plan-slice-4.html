Hivebreak slice 4

Sector 3 and Boss Rush. Goal: more game to play.

A third sector of five waves goes in after the Hive Mind, with new aliens, field hazards and new formation tricks, and it ends in a new boss. Winning a run then opens Boss Rush: every boss back to back, on its own high-score table. Everything stays in the browser, with no backend and no cost. The pictures are concept art drawn live as pixel art at the game's 180 × 320 resolution; the shapes are stand-ins for the Blender models.

Decided with you on 2026-11-03

  • New levels: sector 3 with a new boss. The run becomes 15 waves.
  • New content: new alien types, field hazards and new formation tricks.
  • Boss Rush unlock: win a run once.
  • Boss Rush records: its own table, ranked by score, with the clear time beside each entry.

Two releases

It ships as two releases, each deployed and played before the next starts.

  • 4a Sector 3: waves 11–14, two new aliens, mines and acid rain, two formation tricks, the sector's look and music. For the length of 4a the run ends after wave 14, and the Hive Mind stays the last boss.
  • 4b The Hive Heart and Boss Rush: the new boss at wave 15, its music, Boss Rush (mode, records table, unlock), and the terminal version catching up.

Not in this slice: the online leaderboard, local co-op.

Sector 3: “The heart of the hive”

Acid green and gold on near-black, after sector 1's cyan and sector 2's red.

WaveWhat's new
11The pincer: the formation flies in as two halves from the sides and docks. Warpers are introduced.
12The weave: rows march in opposite directions, so gaps open and close. Menders are introduced.
13Mines drift down through a dense formation.
14Everything: weave, Warpers, Menders and mines, plus acid rain.
15The Hive Heart (4b).

New aliens

AlienHPBehaviour
Warper2The first hit blinks it to an empty slot in the formation, with a short warp-in flash. Combos survive the blink.
Mender3Every 4 s it pulses and restores 1 HP to damaged aliens next to it, shown by green links. Kill it first.

Hazards

  • Mines drift slowly down from the top. One shot bursts a mine and damages the aliens around it, so it pays to shoot mines that sit near the formation. A mine that reaches the ship's row explodes, and the blast hurts the ship if it is close.
  • Acid rain (wave 14): columns are marked at the top for a second, then rain for 1.5 s. The rain hurts the ship and eats bunkers from above.

The Hive Heart

A huge heart in the hive's core, fed by four veins.

PhaseStarts atWhat happens
1Full healthThe veins shield the Heart. Every beat it fires a ring of shots with a gap in it, timed to the music.
2All four veins cutThe Heart is exposed. It launches Warpers and Menders, and the rings come faster.
3Below 1/3 healthDouble rings. The Heart sinks lower, so there is less room.

Boss Rush

  • Unlock: win a run. The milestone that unlocks the Gravity Well, “win a run”, now also opens Boss Rush. Players who won the 10-wave game already have it.
  • How it runs:
    • Choose a ship and a Special as usual.
    • Take three upgrade picks.
    • Fight the Brood Mother, then the Hive Mind, then the Hive Heart, with an upgrade pick and fresh bunkers between bosses.
    • Your lives carry over between bosses.
  • Score: points as usual, plus a time bonus for each boss beaten under par.
  • Records: a top 10 by score, each entry showing the clear time and how many bosses were beaten (1/3, 2/3 or 3/3). It is shown next to the normal records.

Knock-on changes

  • A normal run is 15 waves, so “win a run” gets harder. Records already note how many waves a run had, so old wins still read correctly.
  • The autopilot behind the title learns to deal with the new aliens and mines well enough to look good.

How the work runs

As in earlier slices: I write the contract for each release, Sonnet and Haiku workers build in parallel on separate files, the reviewer checks each round and re-checks the fixes, and I play-test before pushing. Balance is checked with the autopilot bot on seeded runs. Blue numbers run as parallel workers.

4a Sector 3

  1. Contract main session

    Sector gains 3; AlienKind gains warper and mender; new FormationBehaviours weave and pincer; hazards in the run state (mines, acidRain), and their events; the four wave definitions as data.

  2. Parallel workers 4 in parallel

    • Sonnet aliens and formations:
      • the Warper's blink and the Mender's heal;
      • the weave (rows march in opposite directions) and the pincer (the wave flies in from both sides and docks).
    • Sonnet hazards: mines (drift, burst, chain into aliens, explode at the ship's row) and acid rain (telegraphed columns that hurt the ship and eat bunkers from above).
    • Sonnet models: Warper, Mender and the mine in Blender; the sector 3 nebula tint.
    • Sonnet composer: the sector 3 track, in the existing song format.

  3. Second round

    Renderer and effects (blink trails, heal links, mine bursts, rain telegraphs), the sector banner and HUD text, sounds for the new events, and the director picking the new track.

  4. Balance, review, play-test, ship

4b The Hive Heart and Boss Rush

  1. Contract main session

    • BossKind gains hiveHeart; the run's mode is 'normal' | 'bossRush'.
    • Boss Rush gets its records (hivebreak.bossRush.v1) and an unlock entry for the mode.

  2. Parallel workers 3 in parallel

    • Sonnet boss: the Hive Heart's parts, phases and attacks.
    • Sonnet Boss Rush:
      • run setup: three starting picks, then the bosses in order;
      • a pick and fresh bunkers between bosses;
      • the clear timer and its time bonus;
      • the records table and the unlock check.
    • Sonnet models and music: the Hive Heart model; the heartbeat boss track, whose kick lines up with the boss's pulse rings.

  3. Second round

    • Title menu: a Boss Rush entry, locked until the first win.
    • Boss Rush records next to the normal ones; the UNLOCKED moment for the mode.
    • Renderer and effects for the Heart.
    • Terminal version: sprites for the new aliens, mines and the Heart, and Boss Rush in its menus and records.

  4. Wiring, balance, review, play-test, ship
