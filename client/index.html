    How to play

    Clear five waves of the hive to win. After each wave you pick one of three upgrades, and they stack for the rest of the run, so no two runs build the same way. Every hit in a row raises your combo multiplier, up to x8; a shot that misses resets it, so aim rather than spray. Lose your last life, or let the formation reach your row, and the run is over.
